Manual

MicFox 1.5.7 · Windows 10/11 · iPhone (iOS 15+) · Apple Devices for USB video

1 · On the computer

  1. Download and run MicFox‑1.5.7‑Setup.exe. Click through — one prompt. It installs the app and registers the “MicFox Camera” virtual webcam for you.
    Windows may show a blue “Windows protected your PC” box — that's just because the installer isn't code‑signed yet. Click More info → Run anyway.
  2. For the microphone, install VB‑CABLE, a free virtual audio device, from vb‑audio.com/Cable. Run it, reboot if it asks. (The camera works without this.)
  3. Open MicFox. It shows a 3-digit pairing number.

For the camera over USB, Windows also needs Apple Devices — Apple's free app, from the Microsoft Store. It installs the driver service Windows uses to talk to a cabled iPhone. Video only travels over the cable, so without it the camera cannot start. (The microphone works over Wi‑Fi either way.)

2 · On the phone

  1. Install MicFox from the App Store (link coming at launch) and open it.
  2. Tap “Connect to your computer” → “Pair a computer” and type the 3-digit number shown on the computer. Same Wi-Fi. This pairs the two — once.
  3. Tap the connection card to connect. MicFox uses the USB cable automatically when the phone is plugged in, otherwise Wi‑Fi on the same network.
  4. Tap Go Live to send the microphone. Tap Camera → On to send video. Settings → Camera sets the capture quality (720p / 1080p / 4K).
  5. In your meeting or streaming app, pick “CABLE Output” as the microphone and “MicFox Camera” as the camera.

3 · Effects

The effect rack on Home runs a live voice chain on the phone. Tap a chip for its parameters, tap the dot to bypass it, long‑press to reorder. Signal flows left to right.

Troubleshooting

The phone can't find the computer

Make sure both are on the same Wi‑Fi network and the MicFox computer app is open. A phone hotspot or a “guest” network usually blocks device‑to‑device traffic.

“MicFox Camera” isn't in my app's camera list

Re‑run MicFox‑1.5.7‑Setup.exe (it registers the camera), then fully quit and reopen the app that lists cameras.

No sound in my meeting

The meeting app's microphone must be set to “CABLE Output (VB‑Audio Virtual Cable)”, and MicFox's output device (in the computer app) must be “CABLE Input”.

I hear myself twice in OBS

OBS starts every scene collection with a “Mic/Aux” and a “Desktop Audio” source, both set to “Default”. Once CABLE Output is your default microphone, Mic/Aux picks up the same audio your capture source already has — two copies of your voice, a fraction of a second apart. In the OBS Audio Mixer, mute or remove Mic/Aux and Desktop Audio, and leave only the source set to CABLE Output.

The picture lags behind the sound

OBS turns on Buffering for video capture devices by default, which holds frames back and shows as visible delay on a live camera. Right-click the MicFox Camera source → Properties → set Buffering to Disable.

If a little offset is left, OBS can remove it directly: right-click the audio source → Advanced Audio Properties → Sync Offset. Positive milliseconds delay the sound, which is the direction you usually want.

The camera stops when I switch apps

That is iOS, not MicFox: no app is allowed to hold the camera while it is in the background. The microphone keeps streaming, so a call or a stream carries on with audio; the picture resumes the moment you come back to MicFox.
