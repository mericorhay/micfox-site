Manual

MicFox 1.0

1 · On the computer

  1. Download and run MicFox‑1.0.0‑Setup.exe. Click through — one prompt. It installs the app and registers the “MicFox Camera” virtual webcam for you.
  2. For the microphone, install VB‑CABLE, a free virtual audio device, from vb‑audio.com/Cable. Run it, reboot if it asks. (The camera works without this.)
  3. Open MicFox. It shows a QR code and a micfox://pair… link.

2 · On the phone

  1. Install the app and open it.
  2. Tap “Connect to your computer” and scan the QR (or paste the micfox://pair… link) from the computer. This pairs the two — once.
  3. Tap the connection card to connect. MicFox uses the USB cable automatically when the phone is plugged in, otherwise Wi‑Fi on the same network.
  4. Tap Go Live to send the microphone. Tap Camera → On to send video. Settings → Camera sets the capture quality (720p / 1080p / 4K).
  5. In your meeting or streaming app, pick “CABLE Output” as the microphone and “MicFox Camera” as the camera.

3 · Effects

The effect rack on Home runs a live voice chain on the phone. Tap a chip for its parameters, tap the dot to bypass it, long‑press to reorder. Signal flows left to right.

Troubleshooting

The phone can't find the computer

Make sure both are on the same Wi‑Fi network and the MicFox computer app is open. A phone hotspot or a “guest” network usually blocks device‑to‑device traffic.

“MicFox Camera” isn't in my app's camera list

Re‑run MicFox‑1.0.0‑Setup.exe (it registers the camera), then fully quit and reopen the app that lists cameras.

No sound in my meeting

The meeting app's microphone must be set to “CABLE Output (VB‑Audio Virtual Cable)”, and MicFox's output device (in the computer app) must be “CABLE Input”.
