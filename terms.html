Last updated: 8 September 2026

These terms cover the MicFox app for iPhone and the MicFox desktop app for Windows. Installing or using either means you accept them. If you do not, please do not use MicFox.

What MicFox is

MicFox sends your phone's microphone and camera to a computer you control, over your own Wi‑Fi network or a USB cable, so that other applications on that computer can use them as a microphone and a webcam. It is a tool for your own devices. It is not a broadcasting, hosting or communication service, and nothing passes through any server of ours.

Purchases

MicFox is free to use. Two optional upgrades are sold as one-time, non-consumable in-app purchases:

  • MicFox Plus — neural denoise, 1080p video, 60 fps and a longer effect chain.
  • MicFox Pro — everything in Plus, plus 4K, 120 fps, the parametric EQ and noise gate, and the full effect chain.
  • Plus → Pro — a reduced-price step to Pro for anyone who already bought Plus.

There is no subscription and nothing renews. You pay once per product and the unlock stays with your Apple ID. Prices are set in App Store Connect and shown in your own currency at the moment of purchase.

All payments are taken by Apple through the App Store, under Apple's terms. We never see or handle your payment details. If you buy Pro after owning Plus, the app offers you the reduced upgrade price rather than the full one.

Restoring what you bought

Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, not to a device. On a new phone or after reinstalling, use Restore purchases in Settings, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with. This is the most common reason a purchase appears to be missing: a different Apple ID is signed in.

Refunds

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple, not by us — we cannot issue, approve or refuse one. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. If something is not working, please write to us first at mericorhayy@gmail.com; most faults are a setup step we can fix in a couple of messages.

The Windows app and VB-CABLE

The MicFox desktop app is downloaded from this site and is free. Using your phone as a microphone also needs VB-CABLE, a virtual audio device by VB-Audio, which the MicFox setup can install for you. VB-CABLE is donationware, is a separate product with its own licence shown during its own installation step, and is not ours. If you use it, please consider donating to VB-Audio. The MicFox camera does not need VB-CABLE.

The Windows installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen will warn on first run — see the manual for the exact steps.
