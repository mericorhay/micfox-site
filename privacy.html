Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

MicFox turns your phone into a microphone and webcam for a computer you own, on your own network or over a USB cable. It is built to keep your audio and video on your devices.

What MicFox does with your data

Audio and video from your phone is captured only while you are actively streaming (the “Live” toggle for the mic, the “Camera” toggle for video). It is encrypted end‑to‑end and sent directly to your own computer running the MicFox desktop app — over your local Wi‑Fi network or the USB cable. It is not sent to us, stored on any server, or shared with any third party.

Pairing information. When you pair a computer, MicFox stores that computer's public key and a shared secret on your phone only, so the two devices can recognise and encrypt to each other. You can remove a pairing at any time in Settings, and “Reset MicFox” clears them all.

Diagnostics. If you tap “Send diagnostics”, MicFox prepares a plain‑text report (app version, connection‑quality numbers, device model, key fingerprints — never your keys, audio, or video) and hands it to your email app so you can choose to send it to support. Nothing is transmitted unless you send that email.

On‑device settings (effect chain, gate/ceiling, processing mode, purchase status) are stored locally with the operating system's standard app‑preferences storage.

Permissions

  • Microphone — to capture audio while you are streaming.
  • Camera — only if you turn the Camera feature on.
  • Local network / Nearby devices — to find your computer on Wi‑Fi.
  • Notifications — to show the ongoing “MicFox is live” notification while streaming in the background.

MicFox does not use the camera or microphone when the corresponding feature is off, and does not run in the background except to keep an active stream alive.

Purchases

MicFox offers optional one‑time purchases (“Plus”, “Pro”). These are processed entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. MicFox receives only whether you own each product; it never sees your payment details.

Advertising

The free tier shows a house advertisement promoting the paid tiers. If a third‑party ad network is enabled in a future version, this policy will be updated to describe it and the choices available to you before it ships.

Children

MicFox is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data we do not collect

  • We have no servers that receive your audio, video, or pairings.
  • We do not track your usage, location, contacts, or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above changes and the new version is published at this same URL.
