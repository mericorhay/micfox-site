Your phone is a studio microphone.
And a webcam.

MicFox streams your phone's mic and camera straight to your computer — end‑to‑end encrypted, over your Wi‑Fi or a USB cable. It shows up as a normal microphone and camera, so OBS, Zoom, Discord, Teams and your browser just work.

What it does

Phone → computer mic

Low‑latency, encrypted audio into a virtual microphone your meeting or streaming app picks like any other input.

Wi‑Fi or USB

Uses the cable automatically when the phone is plugged in, falls back to Wi‑Fi on the same network. Nothing to configure.

On‑device voice chain

High‑pass, compressor, limiter, neural denoise, a 3‑band parametric EQ and a noise gate — all running live on the phone.

“MicFox Camera”

A virtual camera for OBS, Zoom, Discord, Teams and browsers. Prop the phone up and use its lens as your webcam.

Stays yours

Audio and video go directly to your computer — never to a server, never to a third party.

Set & forget

Auto‑reconnect, start with Windows, and a foreground service so switching apps doesn't cut the stream.

Get started

  1. Run MicFox‑1.0.0‑Setup.exe on the computer. For the mic, also install VB‑CABLE (free virtual audio device).
  2. Install the phone app.
  3. Open the computer app, scan its QR with the phone — once — and connect.

Full steps, including the virtual camera, are in the manual.
